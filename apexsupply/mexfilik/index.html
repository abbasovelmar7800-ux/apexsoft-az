13. Dəyişikliklər

Siyasət dəyişəndə yeni mətn bu səhifədə dərc edilir və yuxarıdakı tarix yenilənir.

1. In short

  • ApexSupply is a service for distribution companies: «ApexSupply Ofis» (Windows) for the office, «ApexSupply Ekspeditor» (Android) for expeditors (sales reps), and the server they connect to, supply.apexsoft.az.
  • The company's business data (products, shops, orders, invoices, payments, debts) is stored on our server, not on the computer or the phone. Each company has a separate database; companies cannot see each other's data.
  • The company (our customer) owns this data. We store and process it on the company's behalf, only to provide the service (section 2).
  • No ads, no tracking of you, and we do not sell data. The apps themselves have no analytics; the only exception is the diagnostics of the Google ML Kit library in the Ekspeditor barcode scanner (section 9).

2. Who is responsible

The company — the distribution business that uses ApexSupply — is responsible for the data it enters about its shops, employees, drivers and suppliers: it decides what is recorded, who gets access and what is deleted. These people should first address requests about their data to that company; requests that reach us are passed on to the company.

«APEXSOFT» MMC (tax ID / VÖEN 7304989091), Azerbaijan (“we”) provides the service: it runs the server, the backups and the apps. We are ourselves responsible for the company's subscription data (section 8) and the server's technical logs (section 4). Contact: phone +994 51 411 12 77, website apexsoft.az.

3. Data stored on the server

Business data — entered by the company's users:

  • the company's details: name, tax ID (VÖEN), address, phone, bank and account (IBAN);
  • products (name, code, barcode), prices, cost and stock;
  • shops: name, code, address, phone, contact person, tax ID, note, price type, credit limit and debt;
  • suppliers: name, phone, address, tax ID and note;
  • drivers: name, phone, vehicle plate and note (drivers do not sign in to the apps);
  • documents: orders, invoices (with the e-qaimə series, number and status), returns, payments, money collected by expeditors (receipts and hand-over acts), purchases and stock adjustments.

User accounts: full name, username, optional phone, role and last sign-in time. The password is stored as a hash, not in plain text. Accounts are created by the company's administrator (the administrator's own account is created by ApexSoft when the company joins); there is no self sign-up in the apps.

Audit log: every change, sign-in and password change is recorded with who made it, from which device and IP address, and when. The log also keeps the old and new values of changed fields (for example a shop's phone or address) and the name of a deleted record. The log cannot be changed or deleted afterwards: it keeps documents, stock and debts traceable.

Copies in documents: an invoice keeps the shop's name, phone and other details, and an order keeps the driver's, as they were at that moment; later edits do not change them.

No card data. Card payments are taken on the company's own POS terminal; the app only records the payment method and amount.

4. Sign-in, devices and technical logs

Sent when you sign in:

  • the company code, username and password;
  • a random device identifier created by the app itself (not a hardware identifier of the phone or computer);
  • the device name — the Windows computer name in Ofis, only the word “Android” in Ekspeditor;
  • the platform (Windows or Android) and the app version.

Every request also carries the app name, version and device identifier: to direct old versions to an update and to bind the sign-in token to the device.

Where: https://supply.apexsoft.az — over HTTPS (encrypted) only.

Session record on the server: that device identifier and name, platform, version, IP address, sign-in time and last activity. Sign-in tokens are stored only as hashes. An expeditor account works on one phone at a time, an office account on at most 3 devices.

Technical logs: IP address, time, the full request URL (including search text typed in lists, e.g. a shop name or phone), the app name, version and device identifier, the company's and user's internal number, and the result. The web server's access log is limited to 14 × 10 MB and older entries are deleted automatically. For failed sign-ins the company code, username and IP address (never the password) are recorded for rate limiting against password guessing. Request bodies (the data you send), passwords and tokens are never written to logs.

Why: to verify sign-ins, to prevent an account from being used on an unauthorized device and to stop password guessing, and to find errors.

5. Office app (Windows)

No business database is kept on the computer: data comes from the server and stays in memory only while the app is open. What stays on the computer:

  • the sign-in token and device identifier — encrypted with Windows DPAPI, bound to the Windows user;
  • the last company code and username, window and table settings, the chosen printer and export folder;
  • a diagnostic log (5 × 1 MB): request paths, status codes, durations and errors. No business data, passwords or tokens are written. It leaves the computer only when you save the «Diaqnostika (ZIP)» file and send it to support yourself; the ZIP also contains the app version, the server address and the company code.

Documents you print or save (PDF, CSV) go to the printer and folder you choose and do not reach us. Signing out deletes the sign-in token.

6. Expeditor app (Android)

No business database is kept on the phone. What stays on the phone:

  • the sign-in token and device identifier — in encrypted storage protected by the Android Keystore;
  • the last company code and username;
  • a cart draft per shop, so an order in progress is not lost when the app is closed or a call comes in. It is deleted when the order is sent, the cart is cleared or you sign out. If the connection drops, a request whose outcome is unknown (an order or a payment) is kept until the server answers, so it is never recorded twice;
  • PDFs created for sharing (shop statement, payment receipt) — temporary; deleted when the app starts and when you sign out.

Android backup is turned off for this app: its data is not included in the Google account backup or in transfers to a new phone.

When you share a PDF, it goes to the app you choose (WhatsApp, e-mail, etc.). Tapping a phone number opens it in the phone's own dialer. Signing out deletes the sign-in token and the drafts; uninstalling the app deletes the rest.

7. Permissions

Ekspeditor (Android):

  • Camera — barcode scanning; requested at the first scan. The image is processed on the phone; it is not stored or sent.
  • Internet — connection to the server and ML Kit diagnostics (section 9).
  • Network state — to know whether a connection is available; this permission is added by the Google ML Kit library.

Location (GPS), contacts, microphone, SMS, photos and files on the phone, and the advertising ID are not used.

Ofis (Windows): does not use the camera, microphone or location.

8. Storage location, backups and protection

  • Server: a virtual server at the hosting provider Contabo. Each company has a separate database.
  • Nightly backup: taken every night on that server, verified and kept for 30 days (backups taken before a server update: 90 days).
  • Offsite backup: every day an encrypted copy of the backup is sent to Backblaze B2 cloud storage (the «EU Central» region in Europe). The copy is encrypted before it leaves the server; the key that decrypts it is not on the server, only with us. Backblaze also encrypts the files on its side. Copies are kept for about 90 days; during the first 30 days nobody, including us, can delete or change them.
  • An additional copy of the backups may also be kept on ApexSoft's own computer.
  • Monitoring: we use the healthchecks.io service to check that the server is running and that backups were taken. Only technical signals go there (for example “backup done” or a short error reason); no company or user data is sent.
  • Protection: connections over HTTPS only; passwords and sign-in tokens stored only as hashes; administrative access to the server only with an SSH key; every change is logged.
  • Subscription data: the company's code, name, contact person and phone, subscription period, user limits, notes and records of subscription payments are kept in our management system on the same server.

9. Google ML Kit (Ekspeditor)

Barcode scanning runs on the phone with Google's ML Kit library. ML Kit may send anonymous diagnostics and performance metrics about the library to Google. This data does not reach us and is handled under Google's terms.

10. Sharing

We do not sell data, give it out for advertising or show it to anyone without the company's permission. We access a company's data only to provide the service: storage, backup and restore, technical support, and at the company's own request. The services that technically store the data are the server hosting (Contabo) and the encrypted offsite backup (Backblaze). Data may be disclosed to competent authorities when required by law.

11. Retention and deletion

  • A company's data is kept while its subscription continues. When a company leaves the service and is archived, its data is kept for 12 months and then deleted; it is deleted sooner if the company asks in writing.
  • Documents (orders, invoices, payments, etc.) are not deleted but cancelled, so that the accounts and debts stay correct. The audit log is kept together with the company's database.
  • The company's administrator deactivates user accounts; all of that user's sessions are closed at once. Shops, suppliers, drivers and products not used in any document can be deleted; those used in documents are deactivated.
  • When a shop's, driver's or user's details are changed or deleted, the old values remain in documents and in the audit log; they are deleted only together with the company's whole database.
  • Closed and expired sessions are deleted after 90 days. The server's technical logs are kept in limited size and older entries are deleted automatically.
  • Deleted data remains in backups until they expire: nightly backups on the server are kept for 30 days, those taken before a server update for 90 days, offsite copies for about 90 days, and they are then deleted automatically. The separate backup files taken when a company is archived or restored, and the copy on ApexSoft's computer, are not deleted automatically: we delete them by hand when the company's data is deleted.

Deletion request: shops, employees and drivers contact the company about their data. The company can ask us in writing, using the contact details above, to delete all of its data.
