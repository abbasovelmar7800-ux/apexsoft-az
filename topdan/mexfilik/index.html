12. Dəyişikliklər

Siyasət dəyişəndə yeni mətn bu səhifədə dərc edilir və yuxarıdakı tarix yenilənir.

1. In short

  • Your business data (products, customers, invoices, debts, reports) is stored on your phone. We do not see it and do not receive it. Copies exist only where you choose: your own Google Drive (section 5), a backup file you share (section 6), or the phone's own Android backup (section 3).
  • The internet is used for: the license check — a small amount of technical data (section 4), the optional Google Drive backup (section 5), and anonymous diagnostics that the Google ML Kit library in the barcode scanner sends to Google (section 8).
  • No ads and no tracking of you. The app itself has no analytics; the only exception is the ML Kit library's diagnostics (section 8). We do not sell data.

2. Who is responsible

ApexSoft MMC, Baku, Azerbaijan (“we”). Contact: phone +994 51 411 12 77, website apexsoft.az.

3. Data stored on the phone

Everything you enter is kept in a local database (SQLite) on the phone. The PIN code is stored as a hash, not in plain text. Uninstalling the app deletes this data from the phone.

Android backup. If Android's own backup service is turned on (backup to your Google account, or transfer from an old phone to a new one), Android may include the app's database and settings in its backup. That copy is kept in your Google account and does not reach us; it may be restored when the app is reinstalled or moved to a new phone. You can turn it off and delete it in the phone's backup settings (usually Settings → Google → Backup).

4. License check

When: when you start the free trial, when you enter a license code or ask for a check, each time the app starts, and when it returns to the foreground (at most every 6 hours while the license is active).

What is sent:

  • the license code;
  • a device identifier — the Android ID (on Android 8 and later it is specific to this app; on Android 7 it is the same for all apps on the phone; it changes when the phone is factory reset);
  • the device manufacturer and model (e.g. “Samsung SM-A155F”);
  • the app version and product type (TOPDAN);
  • a random check number for each request.

Where: https://panel.apexsoft.az — over HTTPS (encrypted) only. The server's reply is digitally signed.

Why: to validate the license and the trial period, and to prevent one license from being used on more devices than allowed or the trial from being taken again.

What the server stores: the license code, type and expiry date; the device identifier and device name bound to it; the first and last check time; the app version. The IP address is processed for short-term rate limiting against abuse and may appear in the web server's technical logs.

Purchased license: when you buy a license from us, the seller links the code in the panel to the buyer's name (company or person), internal notes and payment records (date, amount) at the time of sale. The app does not send these; the seller enters them. The name stored for the license may be returned to the app in the check reply. A free trial has no name.

5. Google Drive backup (optional)

Used only when you choose “Google ilə yedək” (Google backup) in Settings. You grant access with your Google account; the app requests only the drive.appdata scope — it sees only its own hidden folder in Drive, not your other files. The backup file is uploaded directly from your phone to your Google Drive; it does not reach us. Google's privacy policy applies to that service. You can revoke access in Settings (“Google hesabından çıx”) or in your Google account settings.

6. Manual backup, PDF and receipts

When you share a backup file or an invoice PDF, it goes to the app you choose (WhatsApp, e-mail, etc.). Receipts are sent only to a Bluetooth printer paired with the phone. None of this reaches us.

7. Permissions

  • Camera — barcode scanning. The image is processed on the phone; it is not stored or sent.
  • Bluetooth — printing receipts.
  • Notifications — low-stock alerts, created on the phone.
  • Fingerprint — to unlock the PIN lock. Android performs the check; the app never receives fingerprint data.
  • Internet — license check, optional Drive backup and ML Kit diagnostics (section 8).

Location (GPS), contacts, microphone, SMS and the advertising ID are not used.

8. Google ML Kit

Barcode scanning runs on the phone with Google's ML Kit library. ML Kit may send anonymous diagnostics and performance metrics about the library to Google. This data does not reach us and is handled under Google's terms.

9. Sharing

We do not sell license data or give it to third parties for advertising. It is only stored technically by the hosting service that runs our server. It may be disclosed to competent authorities when required by law.

10. Retention and deletion

On the phone: data stays until you delete it. Uninstalling the app deletes it from the phone; for the copy in Android's backup, see section 3.

On the server:

  • the license record (code, type, dates, last check, app version) and payment records are kept while the license is in use, and afterwards for as long as needed for accounting and for resolving disputes;
  • the device identifier and device name of free trials and expired licenses are kept to prevent the trial from being taken again and to prevent abuse — for as long as needed for that purpose (while the free trial is offered). They are not used for anything else.

Deletion request: contact us using the details above and state your license code. For a purchased license we delete your name and notes, remove the device bindings (device identifier and name) and close the license. We keep: the license code and its dates, payment records (for accounting), the device model noted when the license was first registered, and the device identifier recorded for a free trial (to prevent repeated trials).
